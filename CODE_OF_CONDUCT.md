# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in
this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of
background or identity. We are committed to acting and interacting in ways that
contribute to an open, friendly, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

We expect everyone in the community to:

- Be respectful and considerate toward others.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and give constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community.

Behavior that harms or excludes other members is not acceptable.

## Adopted Standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant][homepage], version 2.1. The full
text, including detailed standards and enforcement guidelines, is available at:

https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

[homepage]: https://www.contributor-covenant.org
